About

Learning systems that keep learning.

I am a machine learning researcher at Loughborough University. My doctoral research investigated distributed lifelong reinforcement learning: how agents can learn multiple tasks over time, preserve useful knowledge, recognize task similarity, and selectively share what they have learned.

I completed an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at Loughborough University in 2022 and a bachelor's degree in Computer Systems Engineering at the University of Kent in 2021. My current work extends machine learning methods into computational chemistry and high-energy-material analysis.
